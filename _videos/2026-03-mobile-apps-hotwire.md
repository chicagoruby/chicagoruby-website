Mike Dalton introduces Hotwire Native, showing how Rails developers can build iOS and Android apps using Hotwire with minimal native code. Jason Swett follows with a story-driven talk about building his own continuous integration platform in Rails, sharing lessons on tackling ambitious projects and managing technical risk.
