The May 2026 Chicago Ruby meetup featured talks on sustainable software engineering, problem solving, and Ruby’s expressive design. Speakers explored parallels between rock climbing and programming to discuss teamwork, adaptability, testing, and burnout prevention, while another session connected improv theater concepts to Ruby features like blocks, procs, and lambdas.
